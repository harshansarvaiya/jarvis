# Athletic Recomposition & Combat Strength Protocol (Dr. Eric Helms Engine)

This protocol operationalizes Sir's physical recomposition (Fat loss + MMA combat strength & hypertrophy) under Dr. Eric Helms' Muscle & Strength Nutrition Pyramid.

## 1. Athlete Biometric Baseline
- **Height**: 6'1" (185 cm)
- **Current Mass**: 103 kg
- **Target**: Combat athlete strength (explosive kinetic chain, upper body armor, rotational power) + aggressive fat reduction (~1.5–2 kg/month rate)
- **Dietary Boundary**: 100% Pure Vegetarian (Strict dairy & soy based, zero meat/eggs)
- **Caloric Intake Target**: 2,050 kcal/day (~600 kcal deficit under ~2,650–2,800 kcal expenditure)

## 2. Macronutrient Distribution Hierarchy
1. **Protein Floor**: 155–165g (620–660 kcal) — 1.5–1.6g/kg total mass (equivalent to ~2.0g/kg lean mass).
2. **Fat Floor**: 55–60g (495–540 kcal) — Hormonal integrity, testosterone support, joint lubrication.
3. **Carbohydrate Target**: 210–225g (840–900 kcal) — High explosive glycolytic fuel for compound lifting and striking/grappling stamina.
4. **Fiber & Micronutrients**: 30–35g fiber/day; 3.5–4.5L hydration floor.

## 3. Isolated Vegetarian Protein Stack (~141g Protein / ~860 kcal)
- **Milky Mist Low Fat High Protein Paneer**: 200g (50g Protein | 8g Fat | 16g Carbs | ~340 kcal)
- **Soya Chunks**: 50g dry weighed, boiled & washed (26g Protein | 0.5g Fat | 16g Carbs | ~170 kcal)
- **Amul Whey Protein Powder**: 2 scoops / 64g (50g Protein | 2g Fat | 4g Carbs | ~240 kcal)
- **Amul High Protein Lassi**: 1 tetra pack / 200ml (15g Protein | 0g Fat | 12g Carbs | ~110 kcal)

## 4. Calorie Spike Defense Rules
1. **Oil / Ghee Budget**: Cap total added cooking medium to 1.5 tablespoons (15–20g) daily (~135–180 kcal). Never eyeball oil in pan.
2. **Carb Carrier Isolation**: Separate protein ingestion from redundant dense carbs (e.g. eat paneer/soya grilled or sautéed with dry spices, rather than drowned in oil-heavy tomato-cashew gravies).
3. **Staple Carb Timing**:
   - Pre-workout (60–90m prior): 40–50g complex carbs (oats or banana + 1 scoop Amul whey) to fuel explosive CNS drive.
   - Post-workout: High protein meal + remaining carbohydrate allotment.

## 5. Athletic MMA Strength Split Strategy
- 4-Day Strength & Kinetic Chain Split:
  - Day 1: Upper Body Explosive & Compound (Overhead Press, Weighted Dips, Chin-ups/Rows, Face Pulls)
  - Day 2: Lower Body Power & Core (Trap Bar Deadlift / Squat variation, Bulgarian Split Squats, Rotational Core)
  - Day 3: Rest / Active Recovery (10k steps)
  - Day 4: Upper Hypertrophy & Shoulders (Incline Dumbbell Press, Lateral Raises, Heavy Shrugs, Arms)
  - Day 5: Explosive Posterior Chain & Rotational Power (Kettlebell Swings, Roman Chair Back Extensions, Core Carries)
  - Non-negotiable: 10,000 steps/day for baseline NEAT.
