# Scientific & Technical Literature Synthesis Playbook

## Purpose
Enables J.A.R.V.I.S. and F.R.I.D.A.Y. to autonomously review, deconstruct, and synthesize complex technical whitepapers, arXiv preprints, scientific research, and RFC architecture specifications with high academic rigor.

## Operational Protocol

### 1. Source Acquisition & Validation
* When a URL, DOI, or paper title is supplied:
  * Extract primary claims, methodology, mathematical formulations, and empirical findings.
  * Filter out sensationalized claims; isolate verified empirical data and benchmark results.

### 2. Four-Pillar Deconstruction Framework
Every scientific literature synthesis must address:
1. **Core Problem Statement**: What fundamental limitation or theoretical gap is the work addressing?
2. **Methodology & Novelty**: What mathematical/algorithmic mechanism makes this approach distinct?
3. **Empirical Results & Trade-offs**: What are the verified benchmarks, latency profiles, and failure modes?
4. **Architectural Applicability**: How does this research directly inform or improve Sir's systems?

### 3. Synthesis Output Standard
* Present findings with KaTeX formatting for mathematical notation: \(O(N \log N)\), \(\text{RRF}(d) = \sum \frac{1}{k + r(d)}\).
* Provide structured Markdown tables for benchmark comparisons.
* Conclude with **Actionable Engineering Takeaways** for immediate implementation.
